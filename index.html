EmberMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

EmberMap ("the app") is a live wildfire map powered by NASA satellite data, published by FidovaSoft. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: we collect nothing. Your data never leaves your device except to fetch public map and fire data directly from their providers.

Data we collect

None. EmberMap has no user accounts, no backend servers, no analytics, no trackers, no crash-reporting SDKs and no advertising. We cannot see who you are, where you are or how you use the app.

Location

If you tap the locate button, the app asks for permission to read your device location. Your location is used only on your device, to center the map. It is never stored, never sent to us and never shared with third parties for advertising or profiling.

Network requests

To function, the app fetches data directly from public services:

  • NASA FIRMS - active fire detections (no personal data is sent)
  • Open-Meteo - weather and air quality for map coordinates
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim - place search for text you type
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap / Esri - base map tiles

These requests contain only the technical information needed to answer them (such as map coordinates or a search query) and your IP address, which is inherent to any internet connection. EmberMap adds no identifiers to these requests, and responses are processed only in memory on your device. Each service is operated by its provider under its own privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, theme, filters), saved places and a cached copy of the latest fire data are stored only locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

EmberMap is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Disclaimer

Fire detections are satellite thermal-anomaly observations provided by NASA FIRMS and are not official emergency notifications. Always follow the instructions of your local authorities in an emergency.
